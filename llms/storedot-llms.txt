# StoreDot

> StoreDot is an Israeli battery technology company (founded 2012, Herzliya, Israel, with a technology campus in Irvine, California) that develops extreme fast charging (XFC) lithium-ion cells using silicon-dominant anode chemistry. Its "100inX" roadmap targets 100 miles of range charged in 5 minutes (2024), 3 minutes (2028) and 2 minutes (2032). StoreDot is a hardware, materials and cell-manufacturing business — it publishes no public developer API, SDK, MCP server or machine-readable API contract.

## APIs

StoreDot publishes no public API. As of 2026-08-02 no OpenAPI, Swagger, GraphQL, AsyncAPI, MCP or A2A surface was found on any StoreDot host, and no api/developer/docs subdomain of store-dot.com resolves in DNS.
